How to Connect Google Sheets (2 Minutes):

  1. Open your Google Sheet with registration responses.
  2. Go to Extensions > Apps Script.
  3. Paste the contents of GoogleAppsScript_Backend.gs and click Save (💾).
  4. Click Deploy > New deployment > Select type Web app.
  5. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone.
  6. Copy the Web App URL (ending in /exec) and paste it in the field above.
